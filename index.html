About

I build intelligent systems that see, decide, and scale — with the same care I'd give to a piece I wanted to last.

I'm Amali Akshaya, an AI Engineer and Data Science Associate working at the intersection of computer vision, applied machine learning and backend engineering. I design and ship production-grade AI systems — from object detection pipelines to intelligent automation platforms — for enterprise teams that need reliability, not just prototypes.

My work spans the full lifecycle: framing the business problem, architecting the ML and backend systems, and delivering measurable impact. I care deeply about craft — clean architecture, thoughtful UX for the humans behind the data, and design that respects the people who use what I build.

Outside of shipping models, I'm drawn to the discipline of good design — the belief that engineering and taste aren't opposites, but two expressions of the same rigor.
